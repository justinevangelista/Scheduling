Delivery schedule

Everything arriving within the chart horizon. Booked deliveries come from your Excel import. After a chemical's last booking, predicted deliveries follow its regular amount and frequency, or are ordered at the lower cushion if it has no frequency set. Ideal deliveries arrive just as the level falls to the point where a full delivery fits under the upper cushion (never below the lower cushion), so each tank stays in its operating band with as few deliveries as possible.
